On a rainy night, a young creator ducks into an old bookshop to escape the storm.

The shop is quiet except for the ticking of a brass clock and the rustle of paper.
On the counter, the creator finds a blank notebook with a woven-grid cover.

When they write a single sentence, the ink rearranges itself into comic panels:
an establishing shot of the street, a close-up of their hand, and a final panel
showing a doorway of light opening between the shelves.

The bookshop owner smiles and says the notebook does not create stories for you.
It only helps you see the shape of the story you are already carrying.
