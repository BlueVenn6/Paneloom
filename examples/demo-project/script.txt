Rain presses against the windows of a forgotten bookshop.

Mira, a young creator carrying a worn sketchbook, steps inside to escape the storm.
Behind the counter, an old notebook opens by itself. Each sentence Mira writes
turns into a tiny comic panel in the margins.

When she writes, "The door at the back should not exist," a narrow blue door
appears between the shelves. Mira hesitates, then sketches herself reaching for
the handle.
